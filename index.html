Languages & Courses Offered











At la Academia I love helping students learn foreign languages. I use carefully selected textbooks and a clear, easy-to-follow plan to guide you every step of the way. All courses are completely online and will be one-on-one sessions.

More about me

I am Sorbi George, an M.Com graduate from MG University, Kerala. After working in private-sector companies for more than a decade, I started learning foreign languages as a hobby. I have currently mastered over 14 languages. I founded this institute in 2016, and since then, I have guided students in mastering foreign languages, especially German, French, Spanish, and Italian. At present, I also guide students preparing for CBSE and other board examinations in French and German.

I believe that mastering a language is an art - one that requires passion, patience and persistence. So anyone can learn a language, whether it be for living abroad or just a cool flex on your friends!
